Canary Commercialization · Audience Map

Persona Library

The eight client-facing personas that anchor the marketing audience map. This is the working reference for persona weighting and prioritization.

Source  01 Persona Library v3.4 Categories  End User · Partner · Existing Customer · Other For  Canary Labs, Inc.

Each persona below is a distinct type of person who contacts Canary, with its own motivations, information needs, and buying role. Confirm the map is right, then weight which two or three matter most right now. Where a persona is missing, mislabeled, or one you would deprioritize, flag it.

End User

3 personas
PER-EU-BUYER
End User Buyer
Holds budget authority or purchasing influence. Signs or recommends the PO. Evaluating whether Canary is the right investment, not necessarily a day-to-day user.
Titles
VP of IT, Control Systems Project Manager, Plant Manager, Engineering Director
PER-EU-TECH
End User Technical Evaluator
The engineer who will use Canary daily. Validates compatibility with existing systems. Does not sign the PO, but the deal does not happen without their technical approval.
Titles
Controls Engineer, Automation & Controls Software Engineer, SCADA Manager, Instrumentation Engineer
PER-EU-PART
End User with Existing Partner
An engineer at a larger company already working with a Canary partner or system integrator. Evaluating Canary while a partner is also in the relationship.
Titles
Engineer (specific title varies)

Partner

2 personas
PER-SI
System Integrator / Partner
Sells and implements Canary for end user clients. One persona value with three sub-types differentiated by lead subtype: Established Partner, Non-Program (project basis), and New Applicant.
Titles
Company Owner, Director, Project Manager, Sales Account Manager, Controls/SCADA roles, Business Development
PER-OEM
OEM Partner
A technology company that embeds Canary's historian into its own product or platform. Not implementing for a customer, building Canary into their own offering.
Titles
Product Manager, Engineering Director, Business Development

Existing Customer

2 personas
PER-EC-RENEW
Existing Customer, Renewal
An active license holder returning to renew an annual subscription or CustomerCare contract. Already knows and uses Canary.
Titles
Same as End User Buyer and Technical Evaluator, with an existing Canary relationship
PER-EC-EXPAND
Existing Customer, Expansion
An active license holder expanding usage: more tags, more sites, upgrading CustomerCare tier, or a new project within an existing company.
Titles
Same as End User Buyer and Technical Evaluator, with an existing Canary relationship

Other

1 persona
PER-EDU
Educator
A professor, instructor, or department head in an industrial automation, controls engineering, or SCADA program who wants to use Canary in their curriculum.
Titles
Professor, Department Chair, Program Director, Lab Coordinator
Note on the partner persona. All SI/Partner contacts share the PER-SI value in HubSpot. Routing, sequence, and relationship differ by sub-type (Established Partner, Project Request, New Application) rather than by separate persona. A Distributor designation also exists for internal routing only and is not a client-marketed persona.
