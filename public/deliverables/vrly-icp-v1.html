The Primary Avatar

Vrly's current 13 clients were acquired entirely through Renee's personal network and the ERS sphere. No independent pipeline exists. The avatar below reflects who those clients are and who Vrly is best positioned to serve going forward.

The busy entrepreneur who knows they need marketing but does not have the time to do it, the budget to staff it full-time, or the capacity to manage a vendor relationship. They are also building toward an exit, succession, or scaling event in the next 5 to 10 years and value a partner who understands both their marketing problem and their business horizon.

Renee Mueller, Discovery, March 2026
This dual lens matters. Vrly is not just an agency for SMBs. Vrly is the front door of the Goodlife Capital platform. The marketing work is real and immediate. The advisory and investment relationship is the long horizon. Sales conversations should reflect both.
Firmographic and Psychographic Profile

Firmographic Profile

  • Revenue: $2M to $8M annually. Established business, not a startup.
  • Business Type: SMB in professional services, home services, legal, medical, or large nonprofit. Owner-operated or small leadership team.
  • Staff Size: Small to mid-size. Unlikely to have a dedicated marketing hire. May have an admin or front desk function.
  • Geography: Nebraska home base. National reach through GLC network. Content Engine and AI packages carry no geographic restriction.
  • Marketing Maturity: Inconsistent or absent. May have tried agencies with mixed results. No internal marketing ops in place.

Psychographic Profile

  • Time Constrained: Too busy running the business to create content, manage campaigns, or hold vendor relationships.
  • Results Oriented: Skeptical of marketing spend without clear ROI. Responds to operational and financial framing.
  • Authority Seeker: Has expertise and a story to tell but no system for getting it out. Cannot maintain visibility alone.
  • Exit Aware: Thinking about scale, a future exit, or succession. Makes them a natural GLC advisory candidate.
  • Network Driven: Trusts referrals over cold outreach. Most likely to convert through a warm GLC or ERS introduction.

Buying Signals and Disqualifiers

✓ Green Light: Engage

  • No marketing staffOwner is doing marketing themselves or not at all. Clear pain and capacity gap.
  • Frustrated with prior agenciesHas tried vendors before. Receptive to a managed, outcome-oriented approach.
  • Referral or warm introCame through Renee's sphere, ERS, or GLC advisory. Trust already established.
  • Exit or sale on horizon5 to 10 year horizon. Wants business more transferable. Highest GLC advisory crossover.
  • Established revenue at $2M+Marketing budget exists even if unallocated. Stabilizing and scaling.
  • Google Ad Grant eligibleQualifying nonprofit with $10K/month in free ad spend sitting unused.

✗ Red Light: Do Not Engage

  • Under $1M revenueBudget constraints make MRR commitments difficult. High churn risk.
  • Wants immediate lead volumeExpects ads to replace a broken sales process. Vrly builds systems, not quick fixes.
  • Cannot provide raw contentContent Engine requires a 1-hour client interview. Anchor product does not work without it.
  • Wants to approve everythingHigh-touch approval chains slow fulfillment and erode margin.
  • No recurring revenue appetiteLooking for a one-time project. Does not fit the MRR model.
  • No warm introduction pathCold outbound has produced zero clients to date. Low-conversion channel.
